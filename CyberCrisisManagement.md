# TryHackMe Room Writeup — What is a Cyber Crisis?

---

## What is a Cyber Crisis?

Most incident severity classification methods rely on measuring the scope of the incident against the number of systems or users impacted versus the difficulty of recovering the affected systems and assets.

**Question Answers:**
- First scenario: **Moderate**
- Second scenario: **Low**
- Third scenario: **Critical**

---

## Roles and Responsibilities of a CMT

Key points about Crisis Management Team (CMT) structure:

- An **autocracy** is the best approach for a CMT to ensure that actions are taken decisively without wasting precious time.
- The **Chair** is the person that leads the CMT.
- Executives are usually part of the CMT, including the CEO, COO, CIO, CTO, CFO, and CISO.
- The CMT oversees communications sent both internally to employees and externally to customers.
- It is important to ensure that all actions taken are legal.
- **Subject Matter Experts (SMEs)** play a vital role in providing critical technical information to CMT members during a cyber crisis.

**Question Answers:**
- The **scribe** is the first answer.
- The **chair** leads the CMT session.
- **Legal** is present to make sure no actions break the law.
- **Communication** is the fourth answer.
- **SMEs** are responsible for technical consulting.

---

## The Golden Hour

When the CMT is invoked, the first hour is one of the most crucial. Once the CMT has been established, the very first step is to understand what has happened and what immediate actions should be taken.

**Question Answers:**
- **Assembly** is the first step.
- **CSIRT** is the second answer.

---

## The CMT Process

- The CMT receives updates from various stakeholders throughout the incident.
- It is important that SMEs provide updates in a manner that can be clearly understood by all CMT members.
- The CMT chair will usually limit the amount of time allocated for discussions to maintain pace and focus.

**Question Answers:**
- **Triage** is the first answer.
- The **CMT chair** manages the session.
- The **CEO** is ultimately the one who will be held responsible.

---

## Importance of SMEs

- The CMT must have an accurate understanding of the scope of the incident.
- The CMT must understand what actions are available and the impact versus effectiveness of each option.

**Question Answers:**
- **Subject matter experts** are the answer.
