## Log Ingestion and Storage

SIEMs are typically architected with three components used for searching, indexing, and load balancing. These components are commonly known as the **search head**, **indexer**, and **forwarder**.

### Types of Data Ingestion

- **Agent/Forwarder** — A lightweight agent installed on an endpoint that collects and sends data to the SIEM.
- **Port-Forwarding** — Data is forwarded to the SIEM over a designated network port.
- **Syslog** — A standard protocol used to send log data across a network.
- **Upload** — Log files are manually uploaded directly into the SIEM.

> Compliance and regulations go hand in hand with log storage requirements.

**Question Answers:**
- The **search head** is responsible for searching data in a SIEM.
- Under **PCI DSS**, all audit data must be stored for a minimum of **one year**.

## Types of Logs and Data Sources

### Manual Log Sources

Any log that is manually written or composed by an author. Examples include:

- Change logs

### Automated Log Sources

Any logs that are automatically generated by default from a configuration, tool, or developer. Examples include:

- System logs
- Application logs

### Other Log Types

Some logs may not fall neatly into either category but are often required for compliance. Examples include:

- Email logs
- Messaging or other communication logs
